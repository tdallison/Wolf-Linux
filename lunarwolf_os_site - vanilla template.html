Installation Instructions:

  1. Download the ISO file.
  2. Create a bootable USB using tools like Rufus or Balena Etcher.
  3. Boot from the USB and follow the on-screen installation guide.
  4. Enjoy Lunar Wolf OS!

About Lunar Wolf OS

Lunar Wolf OS is a Linux distribution built on a foundation of stability, speed, and elegance. Using KDE Plasma, it offers a refined desktop experience for developers, students, and everyday users. Preinstalled with Firefox, Thunderbird, LibreOffice, and our custom Notes app, it delivers everything you need to be productive out of the box.

Support & FAQ:

  • Q: Is it free? Yes, Lunar Wolf OS is 100% free and open-source.
  • Q: What are the system requirements? Minimum 2GB RAM, 20GB disk, 64-bit CPU.
  • Q: Can I dual-boot? Yes, during install you can choose to dual-boot.
  • Q: How can I contribute? Visit our GitHub repo or join the community forum.
